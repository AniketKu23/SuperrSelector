Mathematical Architecture of the Hybrid Pressure Model

The SupeRR Selector framework integrates situational physics with Win Probability leverage and Empirical Bayes shrinkage to evaluate true underlying player capability.

1. Composite Pressure Index (CPI)

Innings 2: CPI = [50 + (RRR - 8.5) × 7.5] × WicketFactor + DeathPhaseStress + Closeness.
Innings 1: CPI = 38 + EarlyCollapseStress + DeathAccelerationStress.

2. Calibrated Win Probability & WPA

Logistic Win Expectancy trained on 200,999 deliveries (AUC: 0.890, Brier: 0.134).
Leverage Index: LI = |ΔWPmax| × 10.
Hybrid Pressure: 70% Situational CPI + 30% Leverage Index.

3. Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

θadj = [N × θsample + K × μpopulation] / (N + K).
Eliminates small-sample outliers (< 45 balls) while rewarding proven multi-season consistency.
Logarithmic sample volume factor ensures statistical confidence.
